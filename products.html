MODULAR RESEARCH PLATFORM

From Experimental Discovery to Controlled Deployment

Aurelia BioSystems combines automated experimentation, precision imaging, environmental control and autonomous growth chambers into one integrated research workflow.

One Platform. Multiple Experimental Tools.

The platform was developed as a coherent system rather than a collection of independent laboratory instruments. Growth conditions can first be explored experimentally, measured automatically and compared. Once suitable conditions have been identified, the same environmental parameters can be transferred to independent controlled-growth modules.

01 — DISCOVERY

Automated Seed Analysis Station

The experimental station allows multiple seeds or biological samples to be exposed to individually defined (light wavelenght) conditions.A motorised conveyor periodically presents each sample to an imaging system. Nanometric screws are used to adjust the conveyour under the microscope. Further the camera is recognising the sample automatically.

Images are captured automatically throughout the experiment, creating a complete visual record of development.


Light intensity and wavelength can be controlled for individual samples, allowing different illumination conditions to be tested within the same experiment. Pre-Set temperature is controlled constantly. Day and night cycles can also be reproduced automatically.

Experimental Capabilities

  • Multiple samples monitored simultaneously
  • Programmable imaging intervals
  • Individual light intensity control
  • Individual wavelength control
  • Programmable day/night cycles
  • Automatic image acquisition
  • Growth measurement through image processing
  • Time-lapse video generation
  • Long-term experiment recording

02 — DEPLOYMENT

Autonomous Controlled-Growth Pods

After experimental conditions have been identified, they can be reproduced inside independent growth modules. Each pod creates a locally controlled environment while remaining connected to the wider monitoring system.



Compact Research Pod

Designed as an economical module for parallel experiments and small biological samples. Once prepared, the unit requires only a 12V power connection.

  • Temperature control
  • Water level monitoring
  • Programmable lighting
  • Liquid circulation
  • Control units are inside the pot
  • 12V external supply

Medium Growth Pod

A larger reusable controlled environment designed for research laboratories, growth studies and extended experiments.

  • Internal biological sample chamber
  • Controlled illumination
  • Controlled temperature
  • Controlled water level
  • Heating
  • Controlled liquid circulation
  • Camera monitoring
  • Remote parameter control via WIFI
  • Additional Internal Battery included
  • 12V external supply

Mobile Battery Pod

The largest module includes internal battery support, allowing the controlled environment to remain operational while the chamber is transported or temporarily disconnected from external power.

  • Integrated battery
  • Temporary autonomous operation
  • Continuous environmental control
  • Camera monitoring
  • Remote WiFi access
  • 12V external supply

Continuous Observation

The larger pods include downward-facing cameras that can operate continuously. Images can be captured automatically or requested remotely, providing a visual record of the biological material throughout the growth process.

03 — HIGH RESOLUTION OBSERVATION

Automated Microscope Imaging Platform

For experiments requiring higher magnification, a CNC-positioned microscope system allows multiple areas of interest to be monitored automatically. Researchers select positions on the XY working area and focus the microscope using the Z axis. The system subsequently revisits each position according to the experimental schedule.

Automated Imaging

  • Motorised X positioning
  • Motorised Y positioning
  • Motorised Z focus
  • Multiple areas of interest
  • Scheduled position revisiting
  • Automatic image storage
  • Time-series comparison
  • Long-duration observation


Object Tracking

Image processing can identify biological objects using colour and shape. If the sample moves slowly during an experiment, the area of interest can be adjusted to follow the object.

Controlled Optical Experiments

Because illumination is controlled independently, the imaging platform can be adapted for specialist optical experiments including low-light observations, bioluminescence studies and controlled UV illumination.

04 — VALIDATION

Environmental Test Chamber

A separate temperature-controlled enclosure provides stable predefined environmental conditions for long-term biological experiments. It can be used to validate growth recipes, compare biological responses or maintain samples under known conditions before transferring them to other modules.

Controlled Conditions

  • Stable temperature environment
  • Long-duration experiments
  • Repeatable biological conditions
  • Independent operation
  • Suitable for comparative testing
  • Possibilities for cooling or heating
  • Highly insulated walls
05 — REMOTE RESEARCH

Online Monitoring & Research Dashboard

The physical laboratory is connected to an online software environment developed in ASP.NET and C#. Authorised users can access experiments remotely and view information according to their assigned permissions.



Remote Monitoring

  • Browser-based access
  • Live experimental information
  • Environmental measurements
  • Camera images
  • Experimental history
  • Remote supervision

User Permissions

Different users can be given different levels of access, allowing researchers, technicians, project managers and external collaborators to see only the information relevant to their role.

Distributed Experiments

Multiple growth modules can communicate with the same monitoring environment, allowing experiments to be distributed between different physical locations.

Research Data

Images, environmental measurements and experimental parameters can be stored together, producing structured datasets for later scientific analysis.

06 — DATA

From Images to Experimental Knowledge on a Dashboard

A major objective of the platform is not simply to maintain biological growth. It is to continuously measure what happens. Every experiment can generate environmental measurements, images, growth characteristics and control parameters. Together these create structured datasets that can later be analysed using statistical tools, computer vision, machine learning and AI.



Record

Store images, measurements, environmental conditions and experiment parameters.

Compare

Compare biological development under different environmental conditions.

Optimise

Identify combinations of light, temperature, humidity and liquid conditions associated with improved results.

Learn

Build increasingly valuable datasets suitable for future predictive models and AI-assisted experimentation.

A Platform Designed for Experimentation

Every module can be modified, expanded or combined with new sensors, optical systems, actuators and analytical software.
