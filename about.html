OUR STORY

Engineering Curiosity. Built for Discovery.

Aurelia BioSystems began with a simple idea: research equipment should adapt to experiments, not the other way around. We develop Experimental Hardware, Automation and control systems and Data Science & Analysis in order to achieve coherent and adaptable Research Application Platforms.

Built by an Engineer

The platform has been developed over many years by combining experience in robotics, industrial automation, embedded software, computer vision, precision mechanics, electronics, image processing and rapid prototype manufacturing. Rather than purchasing commercial laboratory equipment, every important subsystem was designed, manufactured, assembled and programmed specifically for research.



Mission

Develop practical easy to use tools in the area of biotech research, allowing high level of automation, AI optimisation and rapid but accurate experimental results.

Vision

To create modular laboratory platforms that help researchers perform controlled biological experiments, collect structured data and accelerate scientific discovery.

Engineering philisophy

To create modular laboratory platforms that help researchers perform controlled biological experiments, collect structured data and accelerate scientific discovery.

Development Timeline

We have completed the most complex task to develop automated microscopy, connect conditional sensors and actuators and provide stable experimantal environment. Our initial tests show that this technological approoach is applicable in many areas even outside of the closed enviroment of biotech research. We have also developped equipment allowing high level of parralelism during the research. We are in the doorstep of fully automated experimental equipment, allowing unprecidented speed and accuracy. At this point we are looking for companies and universities ready to try our platform in colaborative research.

Why modular design

The modularity in our design derived from the complexity of the experiments. The huge amounth of parameter's permutation and variaty of the nessasary equipment was the primary factor in the decision to modularise the platforme.
