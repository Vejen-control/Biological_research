RESEARCH ROADMAP

Future Research Directions

Building the next generation of modular experimental platforms for biological research through engineering, automation, machine vision and collaborative scientific development.

Towards an Open Research Platform

Aurelia BioSystems began as an engineering project to investigate how modular automation, embedded electronics and computer vision could support biological research. Today the platform demonstrates automated environmental control, programmable experiments, remote monitoring and quantitative image analysis. The next stage is to transform these individual technologies into a flexible research infrastructure capable of supporting collaborative scientific projects.

Artificial Intelligence

Development of AI-assisted analysis for seed germination, plant growth, stress detection and quantitative phenotyping using large image datasets generated by the platform.

Digital Twin

Development of digital models capable of reproducing biological experiments, optimising environmental conditions and predicting experimental outcomes before physical testing.

Machine Vision

Advanced computer vision algorithms for automated measurements, object recognition, growth monitoring, colour analysis and intelligent microscopy.

Future Experimental Modules

Electrical Field Research

Programmable electrical stimulation to investigate seed germination, root development and biological response under controlled electrical fields.

Acoustic Stimulation

Investigation of biological response to controlled sound frequencies, power levels and long-term acoustic exposure.

Optical Research

Programmable LED spectra, ultraviolet illumination, fluorescence imaging and multispectral analysis for biological observation.

Automated Microscopy

CNC-controlled microscopy with automatic focusing, region tracking, time-lapse recording and quantitative measurements.

Environmental Intelligence

Adaptive control of temperature, humidity, lighting and irrigation using predictive algorithms and closed-loop optimisation.

Cloud Laboratory

Remote experiment management, online dashboards, secure data storage and collaborative access to experimental datasets.

Long-Term Vision

Future development will focus on creating an affordable, modular research infrastructure that combines environmental control, embedded systems, computer vision, robotics and artificial intelligence. Rather than developing a single laboratory instrument, Aurelia BioSystems aims to provide a flexible engineering platform capable of supporting many different biological investigations.

Affordable Research Infrastructure

Many research laboratories require flexible experimental equipment but cannot justify the cost of large commercial phenotyping systems. Aurelia BioSystems investigates how modular engineering, open hardware and custom instrumentation can provide advanced research capabilities using cost-effective technologies while maintaining scientific flexibility. The objective is not to replace commercial laboratory equipment but to enable rapid development of new experimental methodologies and facilitate collaborative research.
