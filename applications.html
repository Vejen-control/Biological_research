APPLICATIONS

Controlled Environments for Experimental Discovery

A modular platform for researchers who need to control, observe and compare biological processes over time.

Research Rather Than a Single Application

Aurelia BioSystems was originally developed around seed germination and controlled plant growth. The underlying technology, however, is based on something more general: the ability to create a defined environment, observe biological material repeatedly, record experimental data and compare its development over time.

Plant Science

Study plant development under controlled temperature, humidity, light intensity, spectral and watering conditions.

Seed Research

Compare germination behaviour, growth rate and early development across multiple environmental conditions.

Phenotyping

Use repeated automated imaging to quantify visible biological development and compare characteristics between samples.

Controlled Agriculture

Evaluate growth recipes before transferring successful conditions to independent controlled-growth modules.

Food Research

Investigate environmental effects on biological materials, germination and controlled production processes.

Laboratory Automation

Automate repetitive observation tasks that would normally require researchers to repeatedly position and photograph samples.

Biological Imaging

Monitor slow biological processes through automatically scheduled microscope imaging and long-duration time-series data.

Bioluminescence Research

Controlled illumination and dark imaging conditions allow the platform architecture to be adapted for experiments involving weak biological light emission.

Optical Response Studies

Programmable illumination provides a basis for studying biological response to different wavelengths or controlled light pulses.

Biotechnology R&D

The modular architecture can be adapted for experimental processes requiring controlled environments, imaging and long-term monitoring.

University Research

A flexible experimental system suitable for laboratories where equipment must be modified frequently for new research projects.

Industrial R&D

Custom experimental configurations can be developed for industrial partners investigating controlled biological or environmentally dependent processes.

Parallel Experimentation

One of the strengths of the platform is the ability to run multiple samples under different conditions. Instead of performing experiments sequentially, researchers can investigate many parameter combinations in parallel.



Condition A

Different temperature and standard light spectrum.

Condition B

Alternative temperature with modified light intensity.

Condition C

Modified spectral distribution and watering cycle.

Condition D

Different environmental cycle for direct comparison.

Designed Beyond Today's Application

The platform was intentionally designed around controlled conditions rather than one specific biological process. That means new chambers, sensors, imaging technologies and experimental protocols can be developed around the same underlying architecture.
